---
title: Subscribers
description: Collect reader signups with the signup form, then search, filter, and export the list.
---

Your blog can show a signup form in the footer and at the end of each post. By default, signups are stored on the **Subscribers** page in the dashboard, which has two tabs: **People** and **Signup form**.

## The signup form

The **Signup form** tab (owner only) toggles the form on and off and edits three fields: **Heading** (80 characters), **Description** (160 characters), and **Button** label (24 characters). **Save changes** updates the live form; a matching `sites.signup_form.update` tool lets a Manage key do the same.

With the default form, vibecms sends no emails: no confirmation message and no newsletter. Signups are stored as **pending** — a record of interest, not a verified subscription — so treat the list accordingly.

## Send signups to your newsletter tool

Ask your agent to connect the tool's HTML signup form with `sites.signup_form.update`. The form sends new signups directly to the tool; vibecms does not store them, and the tool handles confirmation emails.

For Buttondown, use action `https://buttondown.com/api/emails/embed-subscribe/<username>` and email field `email`. For Kit, Mailchimp, or another tool, copy the form action and email field name from its HTML embed.

For sending new posts by email, ask your agent to use the `vibecms-newsletter` skill for RSS-to-email setup.

## The People list

Owners and editors see the **People** tab: every email with its status (**pending**, **confirmed**, **unsubscribed**) and when it signed up. A search box filters by email, and a status filter narrows the list; a header counts pending signups. **Remove** on a row deletes that subscriber after a confirm.

**Export CSV** (owner only) downloads `subscribers.csv` for use in your own mail tool. Since vibecms does not send email, exporting is how you act on the list today.
