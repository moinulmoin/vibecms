Add `vibecms sites redirects list|set|delete` for exact old-path redirects, and `vibecms posts publish --original-published-at <time>` to keep an imported post's original date on its first publish (an ISO-8601 time with a timezone, such as `2019-06-15T09:30:00Z`). `sites signup-form update` now accepts `externalForm` to send signups to your own newsletter tool.
