# Code of Conduct

vibecms should be a practical, respectful project for builders.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct but respectful.
- Assume good intent when possible.
- Keep criticism focused on the work.
- Do not harass, threaten, or target people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Sexualized or discriminatory language
- Spam, trolling, or repeated bad-faith disruption

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation to keep the project usable.
